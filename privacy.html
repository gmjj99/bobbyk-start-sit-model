The Official BobbyK Start/Sit Fantasy Model

Privacy

Effective 15 September 2026.

The BobbyK Start/Sit Model is a free fantasy football tool run by an individual for friends and anyone else who finds it useful. It does not sell anything, show ads, or track you. This page says exactly what it keeps.

Without signing in

Nothing about you is sent to Start/Sit. The leagues you add are saved in your own browser, on your own device. When you import a Sleeper league, your browser asks Sleeper's public API for that league directly. ESPN is never contacted.

If you sign in with Google

  • From Google: your name, email address and profile picture link - the basic profile Google shares when you choose "Sign in with Google". Nothing else from your Google account.
  • What you save: the leagues you add - league names, scoring settings, lineup slots, the players on your roster, and for Sleeper leagues the league and team identifiers used to refresh them.
  • Where: in a Supabase database (Supabase Inc., servers in the eastern United States). Your sign-in is kept in your browser so you stay signed in.
  • Who can see it: you. The database only lets a signed-in person read and change their own saved leagues. The person who runs Start/Sit can, as the database's administrator, technically see the stored data, and will not look at it except to fix a problem you report.
  • Never: sold, shared, used for advertising, or combined with anything else.

Importing a roster from screenshots

Screenshots are read on your own device first, and never leave it for that. If that read does not add up, or you say the import is wrong, and you are signed in, the screenshots are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to be read once. Start/Sit does not store them, and Anthropic does not use them to train its models (Anthropic may keep API requests for a limited period under its own policies). A count of how many screenshots you had read today is kept, to cap the daily total.

Services involved

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and, like any web host, may log visits.
  • Google provides sign-in.
  • Supabase stores signed-in users' saved leagues.
  • Anthropic reads screenshots with AI, only when the in-browser read needs help.
  • Sleeper's public API is read when you import or refresh a Sleeper league.

Deleting your data

  • Remove a league on the Leagues tab and it is removed from your account too.
  • Signed in, choose Delete my saved data at the top of the page to erase your saved leagues from the database and from the device you are using. Your sign-in record - the name and email Google shared - is kept so you can sign back in.
  • Signing out removes your leagues from that device; they stay in your account.
